# Ambient authority: reachable from anywhere in the process, so it cannot be a capability.
# Applies to every assembly under src/. Tests and benchmarks are exempt -- they use these
# APIs to build the adversarial scenarios they then attack through the capability API.

T:System.IO.Directory;Ambient authority. Open through a Cap.Std.Dir instead.
T:System.IO.FileInfo;Ambient authority. Use Cap.Std.CapMetadata.
T:System.IO.DirectoryInfo;Ambient authority. Use Cap.Std.Dir.
T:System.IO.FileSystemInfo;Ambient authority. Use Cap.Std.CapMetadata.
T:System.IO.DriveInfo;Ambient authority, and drive enumeration is not a capability.
T:System.IO.FileSystemWatcher;Ambient authority; watches by path, not by handle.
T:System.Random;Not a capability and not a CSPRNG. Use Cap.Rand.
T:System.Net.Dns;Name resolution is itself an authority.
